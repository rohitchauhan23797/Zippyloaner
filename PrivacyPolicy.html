Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-27

This Privacy Policy explains how zippyloaner.com ("we", "us", "zippyloaner.com") collects, uses, shares, and protects information about you when you visit zippyloaner.com or submit a loan request through our service.

1. Who we are

zippyloaner.com is a for-profit lead-generation and advertising referral service operated by zippyloaner.com. A mailing address is available on request. We are not a lender. We do not make credit decisions, set rates, or fund loans. We connect consumers with third-party lenders and lending partners.

2. Information we collect

  • Identifiers: name, email address, telephone number, mailing address, date of birth.
  • Government identifiers: Social Security number, driver's license number or state ID number (collected by the lender's form when applicable).
  • Financial information: employer, employment status, monthly income, pay frequency, bank routing and account number (collected by the lender's form when applicable).
  • Internet activity: IP address, device identifiers, browser type, referring URL, pages visited, timestamps, and analytics data collected through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Inferences: creditworthiness signals derived from consumer reports our lenders or partners pull.

3. How we use information

We use the information you submit to (a) transmit your loan request to one or more lenders or lending partners in our network, (b) operate, maintain, and improve the service, (c) detect and prevent fraud, (d) comply with legal obligations, and (e) market our services and the services of partners, subject to your consent where required.

4. Who we share information with

  • Lenders, lending partners, aggregators, and ping-tree participants in our network.
  • Service providers that host our infrastructure, send communications on our behalf, perform analytics, or provide fraud-prevention services.
  • Consumer reporting agencies, including Experian, Equifax, TransUnion, Teletrack, DataX, Clarity, MicroBilt, FactorTrust, and other alternative bureaus, when authorized by you.
  • Marketing partners that may offer products or services we believe may interest you, subject to your consent where required.
  • Government agencies, regulators, and law enforcement when required by law, subpoena, or court order.
  • Successors in interest in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

The information you submit may be sold or transferred multiple times within the lender and aggregator network, which may result in you receiving offers and communications from parties other than the lender originally matched to you.

5. Cookies and tracking technologies

We and our service providers use cookies, web beacons, pixels, and similar technologies for site operation, analytics, attribution, and marketing. Third parties that may load on this site include:

  • a.cnsmrvrfy.com — identity-resolution / attribution pixel
  • cdn101.zeroparallel.com — lender-network resources (ZeroParallel)
  • formrequests.com — form submission backend
  • static.yourembeddedform.com — embedded form host
  • offers-unsubscribe.com — unsubscribe service
  • fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com — Google Fonts
  • www.google-analytics.com / www.googletagmanager.com — Google Analytics / Tag Manager

You can control cookies through your browser settings, through the cookie banner that appears on your first visit, and by sending a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal. Disabling cookies may impair site functionality.

6. GLBA Privacy Notice

Under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (15 U.S.C. §6801 et seq.) and its implementing regulations (16 CFR Part 313), we provide this notice regarding nonpublic personal information collected when you request a loan through our service.

Categories of nonpublic personal information we collect: information you provide on the loan request form (identifiers, contact, employment, income, bank routing); information from consumer reporting agencies and lenders about your creditworthiness; and information about your transactions with us, our affiliates, and others.

Categories of nonpublic personal information we disclose: all categories above are disclosed to lenders and lending partners in our network for the purpose of evaluating your loan request, and to service providers as needed to operate the service.

Affiliates and nonaffiliated third parties: we may share information with our affiliates and with nonaffiliated third parties that perform services on our behalf or with whom we have joint marketing agreements, subject to your consent where required and the right to opt out described below.

Right to opt out: federal law gives you the right to limit some but not all sharing. You may direct us not to share your nonpublic personal information with nonaffiliated third parties for joint marketing purposes by emailing info@zippyloaner.com with the subject line "GLBA Opt-Out" and the email address you want to apply the opt-out to.

Confidentiality and security: we restrict access to nonpublic personal information about you to employees and service providers who need to know that information to provide products or services to you. We maintain physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards that comply with federal regulations to guard your nonpublic personal information.

7. Your choices

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any marketing email we send. You may revoke TCPA consent for autodialed or prerecorded calls and SMS by replying STOP to any message or by contacting us at info@zippyloaner.com. You may request that we stop sharing your information with marketing partners by visiting our Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information page.

8. California residents — CCPA / CPRA rights

If you are a California resident, you have the right to (a) know what personal information we have collected about you, (b) request deletion of that information, (c) request correction of inaccurate information, (d) opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, and (e) limit the use of sensitive personal information. See our California Privacy Notice for the full process and our verification requirements.

9. Residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, and other states with consumer privacy laws

Residents of states with comprehensive consumer privacy laws have rights similar to those described above. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@zippyloaner.com with the subject line "Data Rights Request" and the state in which you reside.

10. Children

The service is intended for adults 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

11. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

12. Security

We use commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information, including Transport Layer Security (TLS) encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "Effective date" at the top reflects the latest update.
