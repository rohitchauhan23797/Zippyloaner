Advertising Disclosure

Effective date: 2026-05-27

zippyloaner.com is operated by zippyloaner.com as a for-profit lead-generation and advertising referral service. This disclosure explains how we make money and how that may influence what you see on this site.

How we are compensated

We receive compensation from lenders and lending partners in our network when we connect them with consumers. The amount of compensation varies by lender, by product, and by the consumer's profile and state of residence.

How compensation may influence what you see

The order, prominence, and timing of any lender presented to you may be influenced by the amount of compensation we receive. Lenders that pay more for matched consumers may appear before lenders that pay less, even if the lower-paying lender would offer you a better rate or term.

We do not present every lender available in the market. We present lenders with whom we have a business relationship. We do not guarantee that a presented lender offers the lowest available rate, the best terms, or the most consumer-friendly experience.

What we do not do

  • We do not approve or deny loan requests.
  • We do not set interest rates, fees, repayment terms, or funding times.
  • We do not lend money or service loans.
  • We do not endorse any particular lender as "best" for your situation.

What you should do

Before accepting any loan offer, review the lender's full Truth in Lending Act disclosures, the loan agreement, the APR, the total cost of borrowing, the repayment schedule, and the fees. Compare offers from multiple sources, including credit unions, banks, employer hardship programs, and nonprofit credit counselors, before deciding.

Questions

If you have questions about this disclosure or about how compensation may have influenced what you saw, contact us at info@zippyloaner.com.
