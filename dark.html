/ 01 — what this is

Find people who lend money,
when you need it.

One short request goes here & offers come back.

  1. / step one

    You

    Fill out one short form. Only a few questions. About two minutes.

  2. / step two — what we do

    Zippy forwards it.

    We pass your request to lenders and partners in our network. We don’t decide. We don’t approve. We don’t price. We connect.

  3. / step three

    Lenders

    Lenders review and choose to make an offer — or not. They set the rate, term, and final approval. You decide if you want it.

/ 02 — one number, and it’s honest

2minutes Two minutes

The full request takes about as long as scrolling Instagram while the kettle boils.

The honest part: what happens after the two minutes isn’t on our timeline. Lenders take however long they take — we can’t speed that up. We can only shorten the part where you’d be retyping the same details into many different request sites.
