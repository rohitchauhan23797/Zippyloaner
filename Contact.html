Subject lines we route automatically

  • UNSUBSCRIBE — remove from marketing communications. See Unsubscribe.
  • Do Not Sell Request — opt out of sale/sharing of personal information. See Do Not Sell.
  • California Privacy Request — CCPA/CPRA rights. See California Privacy Notice.
  • GLBA Opt-Out — opt out of nonaffiliated third-party sharing for joint marketing.
  • Revoke Credit Authorization — revoke credit-pull authorization for future inquiries. See Credit Authorization.
  • Withdraw E-Consent — withdraw consent for electronic communications. See E-Consent.
  • Paper Copy Request — request paper copies of disclosures.
  • Update Contact Information — change your email, phone, or address on file.
  • Data Rights Request — non-California state privacy law requests.

Lender questions

zippyloaner.com is not a lender. Questions about a specific loan offer, the status of a funded loan, payment due dates, or collections must be directed to the lender that approved your request, not to zippyloaner.com. We do not have access to lender account information.
