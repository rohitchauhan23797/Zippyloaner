Unsubscribe / Remove Me

Effective date: 2026-05-27

To stop receiving marketing emails or SMS messages from zippyloaner.com, choose one of the methods below. We process valid requests within 10 business days as required by the CAN-SPAM Act.

Email

Send an email to info@zippyloaner.com with the subject line UNSUBSCRIBE. Include the email address and phone number you want removed.

SMS

Reply STOP to any SMS message you receive from zippyloaner.com. To opt out of SMS from a lender to whom your information was forwarded, follow that lender's own opt-out instructions.

Mail

Send a written request to:

zippyloaner.com
Mailing address available on request
Attn: Unsubscribe

What unsubscribe does not cover

Opting out of marketing does not stop transactional or account-servicing communications related to any loan request you have already submitted. To stop communications from a specific lender, contact that lender directly. To opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information under CCPA, CPRA, or similar state laws, use the Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information page instead.
